# Problem Statement

## Context
Financial analysts, business research analysts, market research teams and business development professionals evaluate company performance using regulatory filings and news coverage. These sources are stored in different systems and formats.

## Problem
Financial data in SEC filings uses varying reporting tags and periods, and company news is distributed across many publishers. Combining both into a consistent view of performance, growth and business developments requires substantial manual effort. Manual analysis is slow, difficult to repeat and prone to inconsistency, and conclusions are often not traceable to their evidence.

## Proposed Response
An AI-powered research system that retrieves SEC EDGAR financial data and GDELT news metadata, performs deterministic financial calculations, estimates future revenue trends with a basic forecasting model, and uses three coordinated AI agents to generate evidence-backed research reports.

## Intended Outcome
Reduced research time, consistent financial metrics, transparent forecasts with stated limitations, and decision-support insights that reference their sources.

## Boundaries
The system is a research and decision-support tool. It does not predict stock prices, execute trades or provide investment recommendations.
